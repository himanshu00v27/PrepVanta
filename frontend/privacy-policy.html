Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Welcome to PrepVanta, an educational practice platform designed to help students develop their aptitude, reasoning, programming, interview preparation, and placement-related skills.

This Privacy Policy explains how PrepVanta ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when users access our website and services. By using PrepVanta, you acknowledge the data practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to provide and maintain the platform's features.

  • Account Information: Full name, username, email address, password hash, and unique user identifier.
  • Authentication Information: Information required for email verification, login, password recovery, and account security.
  • Practice Activity: Questions attempted, answers submitted, practice scores, attempt history, topic-wise performance, and learning progress.
  • Coding Activity: Code submitted to the compiler, selected programming language, and execution output or errors, as applicable.
  • Profile Information: Information associated with user profiles and the user search feature.
  • Support Information: Messages, support tickets, and related communications submitted through available support features.
  • Technical Information: Limited technical information processed for security, error handling, troubleshooting, and platform operation.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Verify email addresses and authenticate users.
  • Provide password recovery and account security.
  • Save practice attempts and calculate scores.
  • Display learning progress and performance statistics.
  • Provide coding practice and compiler functionality.
  • Enable user search and profile discovery.
  • Process support tickets and respond to requests.
  • Prevent unauthorized access and misuse.
  • Maintain and improve platform reliability.

3. Account Authentication and Security

PrepVanta uses account authentication mechanisms to help protect user accounts. Passwords are stored using cryptographic hashing rather than plain text.

Authentication tokens and related information may be processed to maintain authenticated sessions and control access to protected functionality.

Users should maintain the confidentiality of their credentials, use strong passwords, and avoid sharing account access with others.

4. Email Verification and Communications

PrepVanta uses email verification during registration and may send account-related messages, including verification codes and password recovery instructions.

We use Resend as an email delivery service. Email addresses and the information necessary to deliver these messages may be processed by Resend.

Email delivery depends on service availability and the platform's configured sending capabilities.

5. Practice Results and Progress Tracking

PrepVanta records learning activity such as practice attempts, submitted answers, scores, and topic-wise performance to provide progress tracking and dashboard statistics.

Authorized administrators may access relevant information where necessary for content management, troubleshooting, security, and platform administration.

6. Code Submissions and Compiler Usage

When using the compiler, users may submit source code for execution. Submitted code and related execution information may be processed by the compiler backend or an integrated code execution service.

Users should not include passwords, API keys, confidential documents, or other sensitive information in code submitted for execution.

Compiler processing is provided for educational and programming practice purposes.

7. User Profiles and Visibility

PrepVanta includes user search and profile functionality. Information such as usernames, display names, and user identifiers may be visible to other authenticated users.

Password hashes, verification credentials, and other sensitive authentication information are not intended to be displayed through public user profiles.

8. Data Storage and Third-Party Services

PrepVanta uses MongoDB Atlas for application data storage. We may also rely on external service providers for email delivery, hosting, and code execution.

These providers may process information necessary to deliver their respective services, subject to their own applicable privacy and security practices.

We do not sell users' personal information.

9. Data Protection

We use reasonable technical measures, including password hashing, authentication controls, and role-based authorization, to help protect user information.

However, no website, database, or online service can guarantee complete protection against every possible security threat.

10. Data Retention

Account information, practice history, and other application data may be retained while an account remains active and for additional periods where reasonably necessary for security, administration, dispute resolution, or applicable legal obligations.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the platform's operational needs.

11. Your Privacy Choices

Users may be able to review or update certain account information through available profile settings.

Users may submit requests concerning their personal information, including correction or account deletion, through the Help Desk. Requests will be reviewed according to available functionality and applicable requirements.

Account deletion may be subject to legitimate retention requirements. Submitting a request does not necessarily result in immediate deletion.

12. Cookies and Local Storage

PrepVanta may use browser storage and similar technologies to maintain authentication state, remember interface preferences, and support application functionality.

Disabling browser storage may affect login sessions or certain platform features.

13. Children's Privacy

PrepVanta is intended as an educational practice platform primarily for students preparing for academic assessments and placement opportunities.

Users who are minors should use the platform with appropriate parent or guardian involvement, where required by applicable law.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in platform functionality, data practices, service providers, or applicable requirements.

Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

15. Contact and Privacy Requests

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy or requests relating to personal information, registered users may use the PrepVanta Help Desk.

PrepVanta does not currently provide telephone support or a dedicated public support email address.
