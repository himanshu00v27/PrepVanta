Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 August 2026

PrepVanta ("we", "our", "us") built this platform to help students prepare for placements. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account details you provide: full name, username, email address or phone number, and password.
  • Activity data: questions attempted, mock test scores, topics viewed and compiler usage, used to build your dashboard.
  • Your PrepVanta user ID, generated automatically and used to identify your account across the platform.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate your account, save your progress and personalise your dashboard.
  • To let other learners find your public profile by username or user ID.
  • To improve question quality and platform performance.

3. Sharing & Visibility

Your username, full name and user ID are visible to other registered users through the Find Users search. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Data Security

We apply reasonable technical safeguards to protect your data. No online platform can guarantee absolute security, so please use a strong, unique password.

5. Your Choices

You can update your profile details from your dashboard at any time, or contact the Help Desk to request account deletion.

6. Contact Us

Questions about this policy? Reach us at support@prepvanta.in or +91 98765 43210.
